# Post-punk and darkwave

Genre numbers are orientation; the reference wins. Look a named band or track up first (craft-reference-analysis).

## When

Use it for post-punk, coldwave, darkwave and the revival (Joy Division, The Cure, Molchat Doma). Louder guitar music goes to genre-rock or genre-pop-punk; synth-led 1980s pop to genre-synthwave.

## Tempo and drums

| Style | Tempo | Kick | Snare | Hats |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Post-punk | 110–150 | 0, 8, with a push on 6 or 10 | 4, 12, with a big reverb | 8ths, closed; toms in fills |

- Tight and repeated: a drum machine, or a live kit played like one. Few fills; a tom run into a new section.
- Drum machine: the Electronic kit, dry kick, a gated or large plate reverb on the snare (tool-reverb).

## Harmony and melody

- Minor keys, 2–4 chords, one or two chords per bar, often a drone on the tonic.
- The bass carries the melody: 8ths, high on the neck (about MIDI 45–60), with a repeated motif of 4–8 notes (role-bass-guitar).
- The guitar plays a thin line or open chords in a high register; the vocal slot is empty (role-vocal-substitute).

## Palette

| Role | Sound | Edit for character |
|---|---|---|
| bass | picked bass, bright | chorus "Post-punk bass", `lowcut` 120–200 (tool-modulation-fx) |
| guitar | clean, jangle | chorus "Post-punk clean guitar" or Juno I at `mix` 0.4–0.5; little drive; rig Post-punk chorus or Cure jangle (tool-guitar-rig) |
| synth | cold pad or a thin lead | "80s synth pad" chorus; slow attack |
| drums | drum machine or tight kit | dry kick, big snare reverb |

## Mix

- The bass is as loud as the guitar or louder; the kick stays in the low end under it, so keep the chorus off the lows (craft-kick-bass).
- Wide chorused guitar, centred bass and kick, a dark reverb tail on the snare.
- Dark top: a High cut at 8–12 kHz on the guitar.

## Reference core

1. A melodic bass motif with chorus, high on the neck.
2. A clean chorused guitar, thin and cold.
3. Steady drums with a large snare reverb.
4. A minor key, a few chords, a long repeating form.

## Beginner mistakes

- A chorus on the whole bass without a low cut: the lows go wide and weak.
- A distorted wall of guitars: that is rock.
- Busy drums and many chords.
