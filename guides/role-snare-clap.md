# Snare and clap

- Pop-punk: a Rock snare with `rimshot`, a dense room on the drum bus, a clap on top of the snare in the chorus.

## Practices

- Snare body around 150–250 Hz, crack at 2–3.5 kHz. A clap layered on the snare is common in pop and house choruses.
- Ghost notes around a strong backbeat (craft-groove-swing).

## Meloduo recipe

Knobs differ by engine:
- **Acoustic snare:** `tune` (150–350 Hz), `decay`, `muffle`, `strike`, `stick` (Stick, Rods, Brush), `wires`, `tension`, `wireTone`, `rimshot`.
- **Electronic snare:** `tone` (100–400 Hz), `body`, `toneDecay`, `noise`, `noiseDecay`, `noiseLow`, `noiseHigh`, `snap`, `drive`.
- **Clap:** `bursts` (1–8), `spread` (3–30 ms), `tail` (30–1500 ms), `band`, `jitter`.

1. A lower, darker snare for synthwave and lo-fi: `tune` (Acoustic) or `tone` (Electronic) 20–30 % below the preset.
2. House clap: `bursts` 3–4, `spread` 8–15 ms. A wide trap clap: `tail` 250–300 ms.
3. Lo-fi and jazz: Acoustic with `stick` Brush or Rods; soft ghosts sound darker by themselves.
4. Gated snare: a reverb bus with `gate` (tool-reverb).
