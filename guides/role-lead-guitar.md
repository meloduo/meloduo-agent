# Lead guitar

Lead guitar: Lead with more `gain` than the rhythm, even more mids, a 1/4 or 1/8D echo on a send.

## Meloduo recipe

- `pluck` Finger, Pick or Thumb; `hardness` for the attack; `pickPos` (0.04–0.5) toward the bridge for a meaner tone.
- Fingerstyle and bossa: `pluck` Finger or Thumb, `noise` 0.1–0.2 for live fret noise.
- 12-string sound: raise `course` (detune between the strings of a course).
- `position` (0–12) picks the fret area; an octave melody in pop-punk: the same line `copy_notes` +12 on a second guitar channel.
- Space: a 1/4 or 1/8D echo on a send rather than a long hall; there is no chorus (craft-engine-limits).
