# Bass guitar

| Type | Base | What makes the sound your own | Sign in the stem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Picked pop-punk bass | guitar Pick bass | amp Bass grit, moderate `gain`; higher `hardness` for attack | the mids read, the low end does not boom |
| Finger bass | guitar Finger bass | `pickup` and `pickPos` for warmth or click | soft highMid |
| Post-punk melodic bass | rig "Post-punk bass" (comp Bass leveler, amp Clean US 8x10, chorus Post-punk bass), MIDI 40–57 | no drive pedal; chorus `mix` at most 0.5, `lowcut` 120–200 (tool-modulation-fx) | at least 40% in 60–250 Hz, at least 10% in 500–2000 Hz |

- The knobs for the low end are `pickup` (Neck, Middle give more) and `pickPos` (further from the bridge gives more). Hardness and damping change the highs only.
- No Overdrive or other drive pedal on a bass: it cuts the lows. For grit raise the amp `gain` a little.
- A bass stem under 30% in 60–250 Hz at its register means a wrong pickup or a pedal that cuts lows.
