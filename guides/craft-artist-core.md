# Artist and reference core

If the user names an artist or a track, work out what its sound is made of (tempo, harmony, instruments, form, how the hook is delivered, mix character). Take the techniques, not the notes. Split the analysis into two lists:
- **core**: 3–5 things without which the track is not recognisable (for a metal band that is the tuning, the distortion, the riff and the drums, not scratches and percussion);
- **details**: recognisable but optional techniques.

Build the core first and do not change it for novelty. Details are optional.

## Study the reference first

Memory of a named track is often wrong or generic: it gives a genre average, not that song. Before the track card, look the reference up (craft-reference-analysis, "Look it up") and take the core from the facts you found, not from the genre guide.

Do not quietly replace the core with a toy substitute (vocals with a synth voice): suggest what could take that place, and ask if the user is around. When vocals are the core of the genre, it is usually better to leave room for them than to imitate them.
