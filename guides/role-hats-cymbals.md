# Hats and cymbals

- Hats: openness, Foot pedal, choke 1 (the closed hat cuts the open one). Crash (Crash, Splash, China) and ride (bell, ping, wash) are Acoustic.

- Kit hats at velocity 40–90 sit 15–18 LU below the kick: raise their fader by 3–5 dB.

## Meloduo recipe

- **Electronic hats:** `tone`, `metal`, `decay`, `band` (3–15 kHz), `choke`. **Acoustic hats:** `tone`, `openness`, `decay`, `stick` (Tip, Shank, Foot), `sizzle`, `bright`, `choke`.
- Put the closed and open hat in one `choke` group (1–8): the closed hat cuts the open one.
- Ride (Acoustic): `bell`, `ping`, `wash`; for techno more `ping`, less `wash`. Crash (Acoustic): `bloom`, `trash`, `stick` Mallet for swells.
- Accent pattern and velocities: craft-groove-swing. Rolls: craft-drum-programming.
- Harshness sits at 3–7 kHz: lower `bright` or `band` before reaching for EQ.
